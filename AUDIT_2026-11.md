# Self-audit of the v2.0.0 evaluation (November 2026)

Scope: the L-Town 2018 detection and localization numbers published in v2.0.0
(Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.20786241, commit `41a2dae`). The audit re-ran the
repository's own code, read-only, and decomposed how each number arises.
The numerical findings in sections 1–4 and the exploratory runs in section 6
are derived from `scripts/audit_evaluation.py` using the original BattLeDIM files.
The external descriptions of organisers' event types, data units and challenge
setup in section 5 are not independently established by that script; they
require separate source-document verification. Raw audit output is in
`audit/audit_output_2026-11-11.txt`. Published results remain unchanged.

Verdict vocabulary: Verified / Verified with Limitations / Not Demonstrated / Not Verified.

## 1. Training window is not leak-free

The first 40% of 2018 runs from 1 January to 27 May 00:00. The organisers'
ground truth has leaks active from 8 January to the end of the year (`p257`,
later also `p427`). Leaks `p257, p461, p232, p427, p673, p628, p538` all begin
inside the training window; four of them are active at the boundary. The RF
baseline and the CUSUM thresholds were therefore fitted on leaking data.

## 2. What the published evaluator actually counts

`LeakPredictor.detection_summary`:

- scores only the **first** alarm of each sensor over the whole year, so at most
  33 "alarms" exist and an alarm that never clears costs nothing;
- counts true positives as unique **events** (5) but false positives as
  **sensors** (17). The 27 alarms are 27 sensors: 10 matched one of 5 events,
  17 matched none. Precision 5 / (5 + 17) mixes the two units;
- keeps all 14 leaks in the recall denominator, although the 7 that start in the
  training window cannot be detected: the CUSUM threshold is set above the
  training maximum, so no alarm can occur there.

Of the 17 unmatched first alarms, 10 fall within 12 hours after the training
boundary and 13 within 90 hours. Each of `p158`, `p369`, `p654`, `p810` is matched by a single
sensor; `p866` by six.

## 3. The alarm state does not clear

The CUSUM sum has no reset. Measured after the training boundary:

- network level (any sensor): alarm on 99.9% of the time, one continuous episode;
- 10 sensors are in alarm more than 50% of the time, the worst 97.4%.

## 4. Localization

- For all 7 evaluated leaks an alarm was already active before the leak began,
  so the "first alarm after leak start" is the first sample after the labelled
  start (0.08–0.17 h). Localization runs on the first 24 h of each leak,
  independent of whether that leak was detected. `p183` and `p31` were not
  detected by the evaluator in section 2.
- Hop distance is computed on a pipes-only graph, which splits L-Town into 5
  disconnected components (valves and pumps are not edges). "Hop distance: None"
  in the reports means the top-1 guess lies in a different component than the
  leak. Measured over pipes + valves + pumps:

| Leak | WLS hops | Lasso hops |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| p31 | 55 | 56 |
| p158 | 22 | 21 |
| p183 | 57 | 2 |
| p369 | 8 | 8 |
| p654 | 39 | 43 |
| p810 | 57 | 57 |
| p866 | 56 | 56 |
| **Median** | **55** | **43** |

- No leak is in the top-5 candidates under either method.
- The Lasso top-1 guess is the same node (`n752`) for `p158`, `p183` and `p654`.

## 5. Smaller items

- The loader labels all 14 leaks "gradual". An external organisers'
  configuration has been reported to classify `p673, p538, p866, p183, p158,
  p369` as abrupt; the classification was **not independently checked against
  that configuration for this audit**. This claim is not an output of the
  reproduction script.
- Reports print leak flow as "L/s". The flow data may be in m³/h according
  to external dataset descriptions; **the unit is unresolved** until checked
  against the authoritative BattLeDIM documentation. Do not interpret the
  published values as confirmed L/s.
- The 2018 experiment trains and scores inside 2018 (verified from the code).
  The official BattLeDIM challenge task is described as using 2018 as history
  and finding 2019 leaks; this external task framing is not tested by this
  repository audit.

## 6. Exploratory (post-hoc, no verdict weight)

- *Chance level.* A random 48 h window after the training boundary contains at
  least one first-alarm 9.2% of the time. 5 of 7 post-training leaks have one.
  Hypothesis, not tested here: some sensors do respond to leak onsets (six
  sensors fire within 42 h of `p866`), but the single-sensor matches cannot be
  separated from coincidence with this evaluator.
- *2018 → 2019.* Fitting on all of 2018 and scoring 2019 with the same code
  gives 0 of 23 leaks, 24 sensor first-alarms all unmatched, alarm on 92.4% of
  the year. This is a single unregistered run of an adapted setup, not a
  BattLeDIM score.

## What this audit does not show

It does not show that RF-residual CUSUM cannot work on L-Town. It shows that the
v2.0.0 evaluation does not measure whether it does.

## Erratum (2026-11-11, after v2.0.1)

The first version of this audit gave the leak share of inflow as 10.9%. That
figure divided by the sum of all three flow sensors. `PUMP_1` (n54 → tank T1)
is an internal transfer, not a source; the network inflow is `p227` (R1) +
`p235` (R2). Recomputed on the same data: 12.3% in the training window, 12.7%
over 2018. The frozen output file keeps the original line; the script now
prints both. The same double count is present in the v2 predictor's
`inlet_flow` feature (sum of all flow channels).
